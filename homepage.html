Our Airport Solutions

Two connected stations and one control platform that make airport processing faster, simpler, and easier to oversee.

Self Check-in & Baggage Drop

One station where passengers check in, collect a boarding pass, weigh and tag their bag, and send it on.

  • Booking lookup through the airline API
  • Boarding pass and baggage tag printing
  • Weight check against per-bag limits

Self-Immigration

An independent station that verifies identity and face through the government system and shows the official result.

  • Approved, rejected, or referred to an officer
  • Government keeps the decision and the biometric data
  • Immigration receipt printing

Government Control Dashboard

Monitor stations, manage staff roles, handle exceptions, and review audit logs and reports for your airport.

  • Remote device management
  • Smart exception routing
  • Analytics and reports

How It Works

One connected journey, from booking details to immigration result.

Enter booking details

Type your flight number, ticket number, and passport or National ID number.

Check in and print

The airline confirms your seat and check-in. Collect your boarding pass.

Weigh and drop your bag

Place the bag on the scale, attach the printed tag, and roll it along the conveyor.

Clear immigration

At the immigration station, enter your document number and look at the camera for face verification.

Station layout and the route between stations are confirmed for each airport during site design.
